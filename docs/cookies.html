Cookies

This site sets no cookies. Not one, not first-party, not for analytics, not for anything.

What it does remember

If you press the light and dark switch, that choice is written to your own browser's local storage under the name fosscast-theme. It never leaves your device, it is never sent to the server, and we cannot read it. Clearing your browser data removes it. If you never touch the switch, nothing is written at all.

Why there is no banner

A banner asks permission to do something. There is nothing here to ask permission for, and a consent box on a site that tracks nobody is just another thing to click.

The software

A FOSSCast instance sets one cookie, for whoever signs in to the dashboard, so the next page knows it is still them. Listeners are set no cookie at all: the public pages and the feed need none, and counting a download does not require knowing who asked.
